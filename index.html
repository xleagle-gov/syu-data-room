02 · Uptime and deferred production

The wells can make more than they are making.

BSEE reports each well's days on production. A well's potential is its oil divided by its producing days: what it would make running every day of the month. The gap between potential and actual is deferred production, from facility limits, sales constraints or downtime. Wells with fewer than 7 producing days in a month count at actual, so start-up months don't inflate the gap.

Where the deferred barrels sat

    Deferred bopd = potential minus actual for the latest month. Click a well to open it in the well book.

          13 · Sources and limits

          What is public, and what is not.

          • Federal record: BSEE Pacific OGOR-A production by well (1996 to the latest month), BSEE Pacific borehole, platform, pipeline and lease-ownership files, BOEM Pacific GIS layers, EPA FRS facility points, EIA crude pipeline layer.
          • Company record: Sable's 10-K, 10-Qs, 8-Ks and investor presentations on EDGAR. Company figures are labeled as company figures and never blended with federal data.
          • Courts: the 9th Circuit docket (CourtListener RECAP) and orders described in Sable's 8-Ks.
          • Lag: BSEE's latest month is labeled "Not 100% Complete" and runs about four months behind.
          • Not public: SCADA, well tests, nominations detail, LFC throughput, inspection records. Those would plug straight into this room.
          • Counts: Sable's June 2026 deck gives 112 wells (90 producers, 12 injectors, 10 idle). BSEE's monthly status for the latest month is shown as published. The two use different definitions.
          • Approximate geometry: CA-324 and CA-325 onshore routes, and the Heritage-to-Harmony gas line. Labeled on the map.
